<p>What can medicine teach us about keeping machines healthy? Using healthcare as an analogy (with credit to Peter Attia's book <em>Outlive</em>), this episode works through strategy and tactics, why you should only measure something if the result could change what you do, and the difference between condition monitoring and predictive maintenance.</p><p>Condition monitoring asks, “Are you healthy right now?” Like checking vital signs, it catches damage early enough to react. Predictive maintenance goes upstream: like cholesterol tests, cancer screening, and continuous glucose monitoring, it looks for the small problems and process upsets before they become big ones.</p>
