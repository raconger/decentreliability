<p>This is the story of Reliability Centered Maintenance, the systematic approach that reduced maintenance workloads by up to 70% across aviation, military, and industrial applications.</p><p>In 1960, airlines were spending fortunes on scheduled maintenance yet still experiencing unexpected failures. This paradox led to Reliability-Centered Maintenance—a 7-question framework that eliminates maintenance guesswork by focusing on actual consequences rather than arbitrary schedules. Whether you're maintaining a car, home systems, or industrial equipment, you'll learn to stop over-maintaining expensive parts while preventing costly surprises like backup generators failing when you need them most. The methodology helps you spot small problems before they cascade into big disasters, using the same systematic thinking that revolutionized aviation safety.</p><p>The Royal Australian Navy cut preventative maintenance labor by 75%, while power plants save hundreds of thousands per day during peak periods. In 13 minutes, discover how to turn maintenance from a cost center into a competitive advantage by letting actual risk—not routine—drive decisions.</p>
