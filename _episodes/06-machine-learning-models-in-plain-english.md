<p>What do people actually mean by machine learning or AI in asset performance management? This episode explains the three main types of algorithms (unsupervised, supervised, and reinforcement learning) and which of them APM software really uses.</p><p>Most machine learning in APM is unsupervised: train on a known good period, then alert when the equipment drifts away from it. We cover how that works in practice, from residual based alarming to clustering, plus the difference between detection and prediction, the challenge of explaining an anomaly, and why supervised models struggle when you only have a handful of similar machines and few labeled failures.</p>
